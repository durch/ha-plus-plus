CONNECTING
  • Turn (wheel or ↑ ↓) always adjusts what is on screen: the temperature nudge, the brightness, or, with three clicks the same way, a cover.
  • Swipe sideways (drag, or ← →) for the next or previous instrument. The dots at the bottom show where you are: climate, lights, covers.
  • Tap the big zones. Climate: upper-left Heat, upper-right Cool, lower half Off. Lights: upper half on/off, lower quarters previous / next scene. Covers: upper half up, lower half down.
  • Press and hold the temperature on Indoors to save the nudge.
  • After 20 s untouched it dims and goes Home: Ambiance when that light is on or it is dark, otherwise Indoors. The first twist after that only wakes it.
