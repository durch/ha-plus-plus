CONNECTING

  1. The screen is the house: roof tiles on top, the room left, the terrace's two bays right, the garden below. Colour is state.
  2. Click a light to switch it. Click a climate place or a roof tile to open it full-screen; click its top cap to return to the map.
  3. Turn (wheel or ↑↓) adjusts the place you touched last, for 12 s. Or hold the mouse button on a place and scroll: that never toggles it.
  4. Press and hold any place for its close-up (scenes, modes, Save). Drag up or down on a roof tile to move it directly.
