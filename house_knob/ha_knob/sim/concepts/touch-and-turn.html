CONNECTING

Hold and turn: press and hold the mouse on a zone and scroll the wheel (or ↑ ↓) to set it: brightness, a warmer / cooler nudge, a cover up or down. Tap a zone to flip it on or off. After any touch the knob stays on that thing for 8 seconds, so you can also tap, let go, then turn. While a thing is in the centre, tap the centre's left / right half for the previous / next light scene or for Heat / Cool (or hold the centre and turn); Save appears at its bottom when Indoors has a nudge worth keeping. With the room temperature in the centre the knob is asleep: turning it changes nothing.
