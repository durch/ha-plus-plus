One set point, one offset per thermostat

Things this does not cover

  • The schedule switch follows the knob only. Turning the floors off in the HA app or at the wall leaves the schedule running, as today.
  • House mode Away is unchanged: it sets the floors to the away temperature regardless of the knob.
  • One offset per thermostat means every floor has the same gap between Wake and Sleep (2.5° today). A floor cannot have its own night drop.
  • The knob cannot change the Wake or Sleep set points themselves, only shift everything warmer or cooler.
  • The big number on the knob is the Netatmo Oxymoron room temperature. It is display only and does not steer anything.
  • Outdoors has no schedule. Its Off simply switches the heaters and the terrace AC off.

Built today: tap cycling with the 1.5 s delay and the relative nudge. Proposed and waiting for a go: the set point and offset helpers (replacing the per-room day and night helpers made earlier), Hodnik in the schedule, the schedule switch, and applying Wake or Sleep when entering Heat.
